Recent Works: Finality (2026)

This piece explores my own gripes with myself and my relationships. I often find myself struggling to connect with others and feeling truly seen, this sometimes results in self-sabotage. This piece uses many washes of acrylic paint, allowing for traces of earlier colours. layers to remain visible. My work aims to relate to people with similar struggles.
